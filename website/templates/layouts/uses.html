What I Deliberately Don't Self-Host

I run Proxmox VE nodes and ZFS pools in my own homelab, so the natural question is why I don't self-host my email list, payment checkout, or VPN exit nodes on a $5 VPS. The short answer is that stage-appropriate engineering means knowing which maintenance headaches don't add value:

Buttondown for Email: Running your own mail transfer agent means fighting Gmail and Outlook spam filters, managing IP warmups, and watching reverse-DNS and blocklists. Buttondown lets me write in plain Markdown with tracking pixels turned off by default and drops into static HTML with zero client-side JavaScript.

Lemon Squeezy for Digital Checkout: Selling digital downloads across borders triggers EU VAT, Canadian GST/HST, and US state nexus rules. Because Lemon Squeezy acts as the Merchant of Record, they assume the tax calculation, collection, and remittance burden while keeping credit card data completely off our infrastructure.

NordVPN over a Single Self-Hosted VPS: A self-hosted WireGuard VPS pins your traffic to one datacenter ASN and one static IPv4 tied to your billing name, and leaves you managing kernel patches and firewall rules. Using NordVPN gives me instant switching across 7,400+ RAM-only NordLynx servers in 118+ countries with post-quantum encryption across 10 devices.
