Sports & the Teams I Back

Toronto Blue Jays — Scored by Hand in Pencil

Scoring a baseball game on paper forces you to watch the spaces between the pitches—when a starter loses his release point in the sixth, and how a bullpen gets taxed across a four-game series.

I am a baseball purist first and a Blue Jays fan right behind it. Sitting down with a physical paper scorebook and a sharp pencil turns a three-hour broadcast into a slow, deliberate ledger of leverage and decision-making. You see the whole 162-game arc differently when you have written down every walk, defensive shift, and blown save yourself.

[Photo slot: /images/baseball/scoresheet-bluejays.jpg — Overhead shot of a hand-scored Blue Jays paper scorebook page with pencil in frame]

That habit directly led to building the MLB Postseason Momentum Analyzer, the 162-Game Run Differential Analyzer, and the 3D ABS Umpire Pitch Simulator.

Music & Acoustic Engineering

12-Hole Alto C Ocarina & Piano

A ceramic vessel flute has no fixed reed or keys—staying in tune across a two-octave range depends entirely on your breath curve rising smoothly from A4 to F6.

Playing the 12-hole Alto C ocarina alongside piano gives you two opposite ways of thinking about pitch: the piano lays out harmony as a fixed mechanical grid, while the ocarina demands micro-adjustments in breath pressure on every single note. I spend evenings transcribing Zelda, Studio Ghibli, and classic game themes by ear and testing them against the real-time autocorrelation tuner in the Ocarina Practice Suite.

[Photo slot: /images/ocarina/ocarina-closeup.jpg — 12-hole ceramic Alto C ocarina resting on the desk beside the practice suite]
  • Melodic Ear Training. Transcribing game and film phrasing by ear, preserving natural breathing rests, accidentals, and octave shifts so melodies fit within a 21-semitone vessel chamber.
  • Web Audio API & Autocorrelation DSP. Writing browser-native sound synthesizers and time-domain pitch detectors that measure microphone input down to the cent without external libraries.
