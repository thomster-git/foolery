Methodology: How the Postseason Momentum Index (PMI) & Round Alignment Work

Playoff baseball compresses an entire season's emotional and statistical volatility into short 3-game, 5-game, and 7-game series. Traditional bracket views show series wins, but fail to capture velocity—how decisively a club is controlling innings and whether a sweeping team carries rest advantages into the next round.

Round-Synchronized Timeline & Rest Recovery Slopes

Because some playoff series end in 3-game or 4-game sweeps while others go the full distance, plotting raw sequential game numbers causes teams in the League Championship Series (LCS) or World Series to desynchronize horizontally. Our Round-Synchronized (Rest Slopes) alignment anchors every club to fixed round milestones (WC G1–G3, DS G1–G5, LCS G1–G7, WS G1–G7). When a team clinches a series early (or earns a Top-2 Seed Wild Card Bye), a dashed Rest & Bullpen Recovery Bridge with a slight positive slope connects their clinch game to Game 1 of the next round.
