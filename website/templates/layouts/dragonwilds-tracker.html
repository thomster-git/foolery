Prologue: Temple Woods

The prologue acts as your tutorial. It is technically optional, but skipping it means missing out on crucial starter items and mechanics.

💡 Tip — Gather Flax: Keep an eye out for bluish flowers along the way. You must discover and pick up a couple of dozen Flax to be able to craft a spinning wheel and key items later.

Chapter 1: Bramblemead Valley

This chapter is where most new players get confused by crafting dependencies. Stick to the Wise Old Man's instructions and try not jumping ahead too many steps.

⚠️ Crucial Step: Creating the bow and fishing net will allow you progress seamlesly with the Wise Old Man's quest progressions.
💡 Hybrid Gear Recommendation: Since the helmet provides the least amount of defense stats, I highly recommend replacing it with the magic equivalent for your gear tier.

By swapping the melee helmet for a magic one, you unlock a powerful combination of set bonuses:
✨ 1/3 Magic Set Bonus: -50% Stamina regen delay
🛡️ 1/3 Melee Set Bonus: -33% Block stamina cost
💥 2/3 Melee Set Bonus: +50% Crit chance after blocking

Note: By doing this, you will miss out on the 3/3 Melee Set Bonus (+25% Damage to staggered targets), but the stamina and crit benefits generally outweigh this loss. Check out the Gear Progression tab above for a complete breakdown of gear tiers and milestone stats.

Chapter 2: Whispering Swamp

⚠️ Important: Before leaving the boss room, interact with the Dragonkin Effigy to learn the Anti-Dragon Shield recipe.

Chapter 3: Fractured Plains

This is where the old guide breaks. With the Smithing Anvil removed, you now use the updated crafting benches to process your metals.

Chapter 4: Bloodblight Swamp & Stormtouched Highlands

⚠️ Safe spotting tactic: Camp on top of the nearby ruins where the demon's melee attacks can't reach you.

Chapter 5: General Velgar

⚠️ Crucial mechanic: Travel to Velgar's Tower in the center of the Fractured Plains. Drink an Antipoison Potion immediately before starting the fight. Place the horn in the center to summon him.

💡 Pro-tip: Ranged or magic is the best attack style for this boss battle, as the dragon fights along the tower ledges and is extremely hard to hit with melee.

Chapter 6: The Northern Expanse

📍 Essential Lodestone Recommendation:

Before venturing into Fellhollow, I strongly recommend bringing materials with you to build your own Lodestone immediately after completing Step 4. Setting up your Lodestone here means you won't need to traverse the hazardous wither water river and spectral door (Step 3) every single time you return to Fellhollow.

💡 Callout Note — Purifying Fountain: For this initial instance of arrival, quickly follow the path to reach one such fountain. You must attack and hit the spectral tangles until they disappear to be able to interact with the fountain and purify your soul.

🧠 How Dedicated Server Vault Respawns Work

  • In-Game Day Cycle: One in-game day in Dragonwilds lasts exactly 24 real-world minutes (16 minutes of daylight, 8 minutes of night). Vault cores reset after 3 full day-night cycles (3 × 24m = 72 minutes).
  • Law Rune Siphoning: Law rune pillars inside the vault vestibules recharge independently on a 15-minute rotation. Siphon them each time you rotate through for rapid fast-travel essence.
  • Co-op Synchronization: Cores are instanced per party looting event. Sharing your timer allows your squad to converge on high-tier vaults right as chests re-seal and spawn their guardians.

🎣 Fishing Skill & Bait Progression Guide

Official guide for RuneScape: Dragonwilds. Fishing features two distinct systems: Net Trawling for baseline catches and Rod Fishing (casting, strafe-tiring, and reeling). Leveling unlocks vital cooking recipes, base trophies, and game-changing spells like Infernal Rod and Fishnado.

📖 Official Wiki Article →
💡 Getting Started — Shrimp Catcher Quest:
To begin training Fishing, craft your first Coarse Net at a Spinning Wheel using Coarse Thread (Flax) and initiate the Shrimp Catcher quest. Net fishing requires no bait and costs 10 stamina per trawl at net fishing nodes. At Level 3, you unlock the ability to craft your first fishing rod!

1. Core Mechanics: Net vs. Rod Fishing

METHOD A • TRAWLING

🕸️ Net Fishing

Stand directly near a Net Fishing Spot with any net equipped. Press LMB (Trawl). Each attempt consumes 10 stamina.

  • No bait required: Trawls baseline fish (Shrimp, Anchovies, Lobster, Giant Krill, Monkfish).
  • Nets: Coarse Net, Wool Net, Fine Net, Mystic Net, Desert Net.
  • Can catch junk (weeds/stones); level 49 increases catch chance to 75%.
METHOD B • ANGLING

🎣 Rod Fishing (Mini-Game)

Stand back from a Rod Fishing Spot. Hold LMB (Cast) to charge distance. Once hooked:

  • Tire Out (A / D): Strafe in the opposite direction the fish swims. When fish changes direction, the bar turns red—quickly switch strafe direction!
  • Reel In (Hold LMB): When tired, hold LMB to rapidly deplete the fish bar.
  • Higher tier rods reduce stamina drain; matching regional rod drastically lowers junk catch rates.

4. Key Spells & Milestone Unlocks

LEVEL 10 • SPELL

✨ Fishing Frenzy

Enchants a fishing node. Each successful catch grants an additional duplicate fish while active! Upgraded at Lvl 43 with Troubled Waters (-15% fish stamina).

LEVEL 30 • SPELL

🔥 Infernal Rod

Enchants your equipped rod with fire for 2 minutes. All fish caught during the spell effect are instantly cooked upon reeling in!

LEVEL 55 • SPELL

🌪️ Fishnado

Conjures a magical water tornado that instantly pulls out up to 5 fish from a node at once. Cooldown reduced at Lvl 60 with Greater Fishnado.

LEVEL 70 & 99 • GEAR

👑 Angler's Hat & Cape

Angler's Hat (Lvl 70): 10% chance per catch not to deplete the node.
Fishing Skillcape (Lvl 99): Unlocks mastery perks and trimmed cape.
