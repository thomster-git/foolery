Runescape: Dragonwilds Progression Guide

An interactive checklist for navigating crafting dependencies and crucial mechanics in Oct 2026.

Prologue: Temple Woods

The prologue acts as your tutorial. It is technically optional, but skipping it means missing out on crucial starter items and mechanics.

Chapter 1: Bramblemead Valley

This chapter is where most new players get confused by crafting dependencies. Stick to the Wise Old Man's instructions and try not jumping ahead too many steps.

⚠️ Crucial Step: Use your Flax in the spinning wheel to craft Coarse Threads. This unlocks the ability to create a bow.

Chapter 2: Whispering Swamp

⚠️ Important: Before leaving the boss room, interact with the Dragonkin Effigy to learn the Anti-Dragon Shield recipe.

Chapter 3: Fractured Plains

This is where the old guide breaks. With the Smithing Anvil removed, you now use the updated crafting benches to process your metals.

    • 3. Farm Anima Infused Bark: Harvest this from the Anima Trees located near the center of the Fractured Plains.

Chapter 4: Bloodblight Swamp & Stormtouched Highlands

⚠️ Safe spotting tactic: Camp on top of the nearby ruins where the demon's melee attacks can't reach you.

Chapter 5: General Velgar

⚠️ Crucial mechanic: Travel to Velgar's Tower in the center of the Fractured Plains. Drink an Antipoison Potion immediately before starting the fight. Place the horn in the center to summon him.

💡 Pro-tip: Ranged or magic is the best attack style for this boss battle, as the dragon fights along the tower ledges and is extremely hard to hit with melee.

Chapter 6: The Northern Expanse

Heading into the next area north by continuing the main quest line. This is where you prepare for mid-to-late game grinding.

Recommended Set Bonus Strategy

💡 Hybrid Gear Recommendation: Since the helmet provides the least amount of defense stats, I highly recommend replacing it with the magic equivalent for your gear tier.

By swapping the melee helmet for a magic one, you unlock a powerful combination of set bonuses:

  • ✨ 1/3 Magic Set Bonus: -50% Stamina regen delay
  • 🛡️ 1/3 Melee Set Bonus: -33% Block stamina cost
  • 💥 2/3 Melee Set Bonus: +50% Crit chance after blocking

Note: By doing this, you will miss out on the 3/3 Melee Set Bonus (+25% Damage to staggered targets), but the stamina and crit benefits generally outweigh this loss.

Magic Helmet Equivalents

Here are the corresponding magic helmets to pair with each gear set for the optimal hybrid bonus strategy:

  • Set 1: Apprentice hat
  • Set 2: Wizard hat
  • Set 3: Dark mage hood OR Dragonkin mage hood
  • Set 4: Splitbark helm OR Necromancer's crown
  • Set 5: Mystic hat OR Zamorak hood
  • Set 6: Infinity robe hat OR Lunar Garou hat
  • Set 7: Ancestral Hat
  • Set 8: Ancestral Hat OR Dragon Full Helm
