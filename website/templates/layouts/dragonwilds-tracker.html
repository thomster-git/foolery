🐉 General Velgar Boss Battle Profile:

Velgar is a power level 4 boss monster of Ghornfell. He will be difficult to defeat without wearing power level 4 (first time) or higher gear (multiple encounters) and being prepared. It is also suggested that you gather enough sanguine urns so you have enough health to survive.

• Boss Immunities: Velgar is completely immune to Poison, so avoid attempting to use weapon poison. Although Velgar has no specific elemental resistance or weakness, he is also immune to the Burning effect—fire magic deals standard damage, but yields no burn ticks. Water, Air, and Earth rune magic are recommended for superior throughput.

🧠 How Dedicated Server Vault Respawns Work

  • In-Game Day Cycle: One in-game day in Dragonwilds lasts exactly 24 real-world minutes (16 minutes of daylight, 8 minutes of night). Vault cores reset after 3 full day-night cycles (3 × 24m = 72 minutes).
  • Law Rune Siphoning: Law rune pillars inside the vault vestibules recharge independently on a 15-minute rotation. Siphon them each time you rotate through for rapid fast-travel essence.
  • Co-op Synchronization: Cores are instanced per party looting event. Sharing your timer allows your squad to converge on high-tier vaults right as chests re-seal and spawn their guardians.

🎣 Fishing Skill & Bait Progression Guide

Official guide for RuneScape: Dragonwilds. Fishing features two distinct systems: Net Trawling for baseline catches and Rod Fishing (casting, strafe-tiring, and reeling). Leveling unlocks vital cooking recipes, base trophies, and game-changing spells like Infernal Rod and Fishnado.

📖 Official Wiki Article →
💡 Getting Started — Shrimp Catcher Quest:
To begin training Fishing, craft your first Coarse Net at a Spinning Wheel using Coarse Thread (Flax) and initiate the Shrimp Catcher quest. Net fishing requires no bait and costs 10 stamina per trawl at net fishing nodes. At Level 3, you unlock the ability to craft your first fishing rod!

1. Core Mechanics: Net vs. Rod Fishing

METHOD A • TRAWLING

🕸️ Net Fishing

Stand directly near a Net Fishing Spot with any net equipped. Press LMB (Trawl). Each attempt consumes 10 stamina.

  • No bait required: Trawls baseline fish (Shrimp, Anchovies, Lobster, Giant Krill, Monkfish).
  • Nets: Coarse Net, Wool Net, Fine Net, Mystic Net, Desert Net.
  • Can catch junk (weeds/stones); level 49 increases catch chance to 75%.
METHOD B • ANGLING

🎣 Rod Fishing (Mini-Game)

Stand back from a Rod Fishing Spot. Hold LMB (Cast) to charge distance. Once hooked:

  • Tire Out (A / D): Strafe in the opposite direction the fish swims. When fish changes direction, the bar turns red—quickly switch strafe direction!
  • Reel In (Hold LMB): When tired, hold LMB to rapidly deplete the fish bar.
  • Higher tier rods reduce stamina drain; matching regional rod drastically lowers junk catch rates.

4. Key Spells & Milestone Unlocks

LEVEL 10 • SPELL

✨ Fishing Frenzy

Enchants a fishing node. Each successful catch grants an additional duplicate fish while active! Upgraded at Lvl 43 with Troubled Waters (-15% fish stamina).

LEVEL 30 • SPELL

🔥 Infernal Rod

Enchants your equipped rod with fire for 2 minutes. All fish caught during the spell effect are instantly cooked upon reeling in!

LEVEL 55 • SPELL

🌪️ Fishnado

Conjures a magical water tornado that instantly pulls out up to 5 fish from a node at once. Cooldown reduced at Lvl 60 with Greater Fishnado.

LEVEL 70 & 99 • GEAR

👑 Angler's Hat & Cape

Angler's Hat (Lvl 70): 10% chance per catch not to deplete the node.
Fishing Skillcape (Lvl 99): Unlocks mastery perks and trimmed cape.
