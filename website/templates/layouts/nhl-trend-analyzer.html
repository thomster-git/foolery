Methodology: Tracking Hockey Momentum

The NHL Trend Analyzer visualizes team performance over the course of the 82-game regular season. Unlike traditional standings that only show the current state of a team's points, this tool reveals the trajectory and momentum of how they got there.

Cumulative Goal Differential

A team's goal differential (goals scored minus goals allowed) is historically one of the strongest predictive indicators of future success. Teams that win many one-goal games but have a negative or flat goal differential are often statistical anomalies primed for regression. Conversely, teams that consistently outshoot and outscore opponents but suffer from poor luck in overtime shootouts will typically bounce back.

Points Percentage Trajectory

The "Points Percentage" metric tracks the ratio of points earned versus total points available at any given game in the season. A perfectly average team will hover around 0.500 (colloquially the "NHL .500" mark, though the loser point inflates this). Monitoring this curve allows you to see distinct coaching changes, injury impacts, and momentum swings that raw standings conceal.
