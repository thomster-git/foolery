Methodology: How This Tool Works

The Autocorrelation Tuner

The live microphone tuner uses the Web Audio API and a mathematical algorithm called autocorrelation to detect pitch. By sampling the raw audio waveform in real-time and comparing it to delayed versions of itself, the algorithm identifies the fundamental frequency of the note being played. This is far more accurate for wind instruments than simple Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT), allowing the tuner to provide sub-cent accuracy even with the natural breathy timbre of an ocarina.

Ocarina Fingering Architecture

The fingering charts are designed specifically for a 12-hole Alto or Tenor C Ocarina. Unlike linear instruments like a piano, the ocarina is a vessel flute. Pitch is determined primarily by the total surface area of the open holes relative to the internal volume of the chamber, rather than the specific placement of the holes. However, cross-fingerings and sub-holes (the two small holes used for accidentals) provide precise intonation adjustments.
