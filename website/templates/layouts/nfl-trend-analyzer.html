Methodology: Tracking NFL True Performance

The NFL Trend Analyzer visualizes team performance over the 17-game regular season. Because the NFL season is so short compared to the MLB or NBA, single-game variance (turnovers, bad bounces, officiating) can drastically skew a team's win-loss record.

Cumulative Point Differential

A team's point differential (points scored minus points allowed) is one of the most reliable indicators of how good they actually are. A team that is 9-3 but has a negative point differential has likely been exceptionally lucky in one-score games, and is a prime candidate for negative regression in the playoffs.

Points Allowed vs Points Scored

By splitting the differential into its component parts, you can visualize the specific identity of a team. Are they winning shootouts, or grinding out defensive struggles? Tracking this on a weekly basis reveals momentum shifts triggered by injuries to key defensive personnel or changes in offensive play-calling.
