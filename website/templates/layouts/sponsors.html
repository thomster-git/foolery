Sponsorships & Partnerships

Whether you're looking for a featured spot in the Library of Recommendations or dedicated ad space on the site, here's how we can work together.

1. The Library of Recommendations

The Library is a heavily curated collection of tools, services, and products that I personally use and vouch for. It's not a generic directory. If your product is excellent and you want a chance to be included, you can pitch it to me.

Important: You cannot buy your way into the Library. Inclusion is based entirely on merit. However, you can sponsor the site (see below) which guarantees your product is reviewed and considered.

2. Sponsored Advertisements

If you want guaranteed visibility, you can purchase sponsored ad space. This space replaces the standard placeholders in the sidebar and at the end of articles.

  • Sidebar Banner: Displayed globally next to all articles and interactive tools.
  • In-Feed Mention: A dedicated, transparently labeled shout-out within the body of high-traffic essays or projects.

3. The Shit List

If you mess around — whether that's terrible customer service, anti-consumer practices, or selling broken garbage — you get put on The Shit List. Obviously, you cannot buy advertisements to get off the Shit List, nor will I run ads for companies currently on it.
