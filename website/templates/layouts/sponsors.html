Creative Specifications & Guidelines

Primary Newsletter Specs

  • Headline: Max 60 characters
  • Body Copy: 60 to 80 words (markdown supported)
  • Image/Logo: 600×300 max (PNG, JPG, or SVG, < 200KB)
  • CTA Button: Up to 25 characters (e.g. "Try Free for 14 Days")
  • Links: Standard URLs with custom UTM parameters

Sidebar Banner Specs

  • Dimensions: 300×150 px or 300×250 px
  • Formats: SVG, WebP, PNG, or styled HTML text
  • Max File Size: 150KB (lightweight & fast-loading)
  • Design Aesthetic: Clean, high-contrast, dark-mode friendly
  • Target: Opens directly in new tab (dofollow or sponsor rel)

Our Sponsorship Principles

Trust is our highest value. We operate under strict transparency rules to protect our readers and our partners.

Sponsorship does not buy favorable reviews, opinions, or editorial steering. Our writing remains completely independent.

We never inject third-party spyware, tracking cookies, or tracking pixels into our readers' browsers or email inboxes.

We personally vet every sponsor. We only partner with products and creators we would happily recommend to colleagues.
