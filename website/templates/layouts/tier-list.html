How It Works
  • Clipboard Pasting: Take a screenshot using your OS shortcut (Win+Shift+S on Windows or Cmd+Shift+4 on Mac), click anywhere on this page, and press Ctrl+V. The screenshot is immediately clipped into an option tile in the stash.
  • Dynamic Natural Expansion: Drop as many items as you want into any tier. Rows expand downward automatically to accommodate growing categories without clipping or cramped scrollbars.
  • Customization: Click any tier label to rename it, or use the row menu to change colors, reorder rows up/down, or insert new tiers.
  • Instant Export: Click Export PNG to download a high-res branded graphic, or Copy Image to paste directly into Discord, Reddit, or Twitter.
