About

I’m an IT consultant and workflow designer who likes to start where things “kind of work” but nobody can explain how.

My background is a mix of mechanical engineering, mining operations, and hands-on IT. I’ve built and run infrastructure at a remote fluorspar mine site, selected and swapped critical vendors (MSPs, LMS, ERP hosting, Palo Alto firewalls), led recovery through an Akira ransomware incident, and designed white-box servers with on-shelf warm spares where big-vendor SLA contracts didn’t match reality.

If your KPIs are so brittle that people feel pressured to fudge numbers instead of explaining them, the measurement system is broken.

These days, I help small teams turn undocumented chaos into clear, maintainable systems: mapping how work actually flows, turning that into decision trees and runbooks, and building the lightweight tooling (Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Automate, Proxmox VE, Python/PowerShell) around it.

Outside client work, I keep my hands dirty with a Proxmox homelab, custom PC and server builds, and a laser engraving workshop (EtchCentric Creations).
