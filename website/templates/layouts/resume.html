About

I’m an IT consultant and workflow designer who likes to start where things “kind of work” but nobody can explain how.

My background is a mix of engineering, mining operations, and IT. I’ve built and run infrastructure at a remote mine site, selected and swapped critical vendors (MSPs, LMS, ERP hosting, firewalls), and designed white-box servers and networks where big-vendor support models simply didn’t make sense.

These days, I help small teams turn that kind of chaos into clear, documented systems:

  • Mapping how work actually flows, not how the procedure says it should.
  • Turning that into decision trees, flowcharts, and “who does what when” diagrams.
  • Building the IT around it: SharePoint structures, Power Automate flows, scripts, and dashboards.

I care a lot about metrics that work for you instead of against you. If your KPIs are so brittle that people feel pressured to fudge numbers instead of explaining them, the system is broken. I’d rather help you design measures that reflect reality and give you a clear story to tell.

Outside client work, I keep my hands dirty with a homelab, custom PC and server builds, and a laser engraving studio (EtchCentric) that lets me play with design, production workflows, and small-business process in the real world.
