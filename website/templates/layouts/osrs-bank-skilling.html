About This OSRS Bank Skilling Profit Engine

Bank standing skills in Old School RuneScape (Fletching, Herblore, Crafting, Magic, Smithing, and Cooking) often represent massive GP drains for casual players. However, because high-level players pay a premium for fast XP, intermediate steps (like stringing bows, cutting uncut gems, cleaning grimy herbs, or making unfinished potions) frequently offer substantial profit margins for players willing to process materials at the Grand Exchange.

2% GE Tax Accuracy: When selling crafted items back to the Grand Exchange, the tool automatically calculates the 2% tax (capped at 5M GP). For items converted via High Alchemy or shop sales, 0% tax is applied.
GP / XP Efficiency: Positive GP/XP numbers in green indicate methods where you make money while gaining skill XP. A negative number indicates the exact GP cost per XP gained.
4-Hour GE Limits: The Grand Exchange enforces strict 4-hour buy quotas. The "4h Limit" column reveals your maximum theoretical profit per 4-hour resting cycle before you must switch items.
