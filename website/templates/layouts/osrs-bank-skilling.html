Grand Exchange Execution & Listing Guide

When crafting or flipping on the Grand Exchange, rounded numbers like "1.4K" or "1.8M" cause costly pricing errors. This engine provides the full unrounded GP price for every step:

Exact Grand Exchange Listing Price: The GE Listing Price column shows the exact integer price to enter into the Grand Exchange sell window. The 2% GE tax (capped at 5,000,000 GP) is shown right beneath it so you see both your gross listing price and your net cash receipt.
Overnight "Sleep Flips": People frequently dump high-volume consumables and popular gear at the lowest current bid when logging off. By placing low-ball buy orders overnight, you snag these temporary price dips. Over 6–8 hours of sleep, you also bypass the 4-hour GE limit to collect up to 2 full allocations.
Zero-Tax High Alchemy: Items converted via High Alchemy bypass the Grand Exchange entirely, avoiding the 2% tax. When high-volume crafted items dip below alch price minus nature rune cost, alching them guarantees 100% risk-free profit.
