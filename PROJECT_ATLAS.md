# Purpose

Project Atlas is an open, long-term software platform for creating interconnected knowledge websites.

Its first implementation is **Thoms Foolery**, a personal digital garden exploring technology, creativity, nostalgia, and personal growth through meaningful relationships between ideas.

Atlas combines philosophy, architecture, content modelling, design systems, software engineering, and storytelling into a single cohesive platform.

Rather than treating webpages as isolated destinations, Atlas treats knowledge as a connected network.

Every component exists to support one goal:

> **Create digital spaces where exploration is more rewarding than navigation.**

---

# Philosophy

Project Atlas is built upon one simple belief.

> **Everything is connected.**

Technology.

Creativity.

Stories.

Projects.

Ideas.

Memories.

Growth.

Knowledge becomes more valuable when its relationships are visible.

Rather than organizing information into isolated categories, Atlas is designed to reveal meaningful connections across every topic.

Visitors should leave understanding not only the subject they came for, but also the unexpected ideas surrounding it.

Connections are not accidental.

They are the product.

---

# Core Principles

Every architectural, design, and engineering decision should reinforce the following principles.

1. Everything is connected.
2. Exploration over navigation.
3. Connections over categories.
4. Storytelling over blogging.
5. Accessibility by default.
6. Simplicity over unnecessary complexity.
7. Design for longevity.

These principles define Atlas more than any specific technology ever will.

---

# Scope

Project Atlas defines how interconnected knowledge platforms are envisioned, designed, engineered, documented, and maintained.

Today, Atlas powers **Thoms Foolery**.

Tomorrow, Atlas may power many different digital gardens while remaining faithful to the same underlying philosophy.

This document serves as the project's constitution.

Detailed specifications are maintained throughout the documentation.

---

# Atlas Ecosystem

Project Atlas currently consists of four major systems.

| System | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Atlas Blueprint** | Defines the philosophy, vision, documentation, and long-term direction of the project. |
| **Atlas Core** | The software engine responsible for transforming structured knowledge into interactive experiences. |
| **Thoms Foolery** | The first public implementation and showcase of Atlas. |
| **Atlas Framework** *(Future)* | A reusable platform allowing anyone to build interconnected digital gardens. |

Future versions may introduce **Atlas Studio**, a visual authoring environment for creating and maintaining Atlas-powered websites.

# Long-Term Vision

Atlas is intentionally designed to grow alongside its creator.

What begins as the software powering **Thoms Foolery** may eventually evolve into a reusable platform that enables anyone to create their own interconnected digital garden.

Every decision made today should make that future easier, not harder.

---

# Relationship to Thoms Foolery

Thoms Foolery is not separate from Atlas.

It is Atlas's first implementation.

New architectural ideas should be explored through Thoms Foolery before becoming part of the broader Atlas platform.

In this way, the website serves as both a personal digital home and a living demonstration of the software that powers it.
