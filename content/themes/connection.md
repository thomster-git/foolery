The core manifesto behind this project is that everything is connected — not as a spiritual claim, but as a practical observation. The decision I made to set up immutable backups at the mining company didn't happen in a vacuum: it came from a prior context, a set of things I'd read, a particular way of thinking I'd developed from engineering training. When the ransomware hit, that earlier decision determined the outcome. What looks like a single event in the present is always a product of every circumstance that came before it. Once you start seeing things that way, it becomes very hard to treat any decision as isolated or any moment as self-contained.

Standing in the crowd at a Billy Talent show and crying — that was connection too. Not to the people around me, who didn't know it was happening, but to something the music was doing that I couldn't have accessed any other way. Music is one of the few channels that bypasses whatever filtering I do and reaches something more direct. When the burnout took it away — when I went from 150,000 minutes a year to almost nothing — what I lost wasn't entertainment, it was a form of contact with my own emotional state. The slow return of music has felt less like recovery and more like reconnection. To something I'd been cut off from rather than something I'd simply lost interest in.

In the NICU, waiting for news about my son, I wanted someone who understood what it was like to process a medical emergency while being autistic — the sensory overload of the environment, the way uncertainty compounds when your brain runs worst-case scenarios in parallel and can't stop. There was no one like that available. That gap — the absence of autistic-specific support in a moment when it would have mattered enormously — is part of why connection as a theme isn't abstract for me. It's not about networking or community in the generic sense. It's about whether the structures around you are built to actually reach you, or whether you're left to make do with infrastructure designed for someone else.
