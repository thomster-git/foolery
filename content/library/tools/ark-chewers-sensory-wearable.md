# ARK Therapeutic Oral Motor Chewers

> **Discreet, durable oral stimming and motor regulation tools.**

Oral stimming is a common self-regulation mechanism for neurodivergent individuals. ARK Therapeutic produces medical-grade, non-toxic oral sensory chewables in various shapes and toughness levels.

## Variants & Models

- **Light Saber Wearable Pendant**: Discreet wearable necklace for on-the-go regulation.
- **Y-Chew**: Ergonomic three-arm design providing varied textures and resistance levels for molars.
- **Textured Grabber**: Tactile sensory surfaces for proprioceptive jaw input and stress relief.
