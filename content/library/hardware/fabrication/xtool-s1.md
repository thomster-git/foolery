The xTool S1 is an enclosed diode laser cutter and engraver. Unlike traditional CO2 or fiber lasers, a diode laser creates its beam directly using electricity. 

I picked this specific model for my fabrication setup due to a few key technical features:
- **Wide Bed:** It offers a massive working area for larger projects.
- **Interchangeable Modules:** It has the ability to easily swap to an IR laser module, which is crucial for engraving raw metals.
- **40W Power:** The 40W diode laser module can cut through some impressively thick materials, performing comparably to—or in some cases better than—lower-end CO2 lasers on the market.
- **Enclosed Safety:** Not having to wear safety glasses while the machine is running, thanks to its protective top cover and safety interlocks, is a massive quality-of-life perk for long jobs.

### Software Configuration

When it comes to software, the native xTool Creative Space (XCS) is very user-friendly. However, it doesn't work natively on Fedora Linux. 

Because of this—and because I treat my fabrication as a serious endeavor—I use **LightBurn**. While XCS is great for beginners, if you’re using your laser for a business or at a commercial level, you’ll want to get used to the power and complexity of LightBurn anyway.
