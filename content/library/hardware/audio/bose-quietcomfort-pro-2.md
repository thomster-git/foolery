# Bose QuietComfort Pro 2 Noise-Canceling Headphones

> **Essential sensory regulation gear for auditory isolation.**

Active noise-canceling (ANC) headphones are a foundational tool for managing auditory sensory overload. The Bose QuietComfort series provides industry-leading noise reduction, allowing for focus during travel, crowded environments, or high-stimulation workdays.

## Key Uses & Sensory Benefits

- **Auditory Isolation**: Blocks background chatter, low-frequency hums, and unpredictable environmental noise.
- **Active Mode Equalization**: The active mode is incredible for equalizing sharp, sudden noises. A piercing child's squeal gets leveled out to the same volume as their talking voice.
- **Burnout Mitigation**: Prevents sensory energy drain during travel and public outings.
- **Focus & Deep Work**: Pair with binaural beats or dark noise for undisturbed writing and coding sessions.
