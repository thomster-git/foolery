# Bo Burnham

Bo Burnham is an American comedian, musician, filmmaker, and actor. He gained early fame on YouTube before creating acclaimed comedy specials and directing the coming-of-age film *Eighth Grade*. His 2021 special *Inside* explored isolation, digital culture, and mental health during global lockdowns.
