# Billy Talent

Billy Talent is a Canadian rock band formed in 1993 in Mississauga, Ontario. The band consists of Benjamin Kowalewicz, Ian D'Sa, Jonathan Gallant, and Aaron Solowoniuk (with Jordan Hastings touring). Known for their multi-platinum albums *Billy Talent I*, *II*, and *III*, the band remains a cornerstone of Canadian punk rock music.
