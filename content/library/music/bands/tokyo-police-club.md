# Tokyo Police Club

Introduced to me by a friend in 2011 during my post-secondary years. Like many of my favorite artists, their first two albums resonate with me the most. I still prefer listening to *Champ* completely front-to-back. It serves as a distinct chronological anchor for that specific era of my life.
