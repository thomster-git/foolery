# Green Day

My Grade 7 "punk awakening." After deciding Simple Plan wasn't cool enough, I pivoted to Green Day. I managed to buy *Dookie* because it miraculously didn't have a warning sticker (which I realized on the drive home after reading the lyric book, promptly hiding it from my parents). 

I also burned two copies of *American Idiot* — one live, and one studio version that I accidentally downloaded as the censored cut, which drove me crazy.
