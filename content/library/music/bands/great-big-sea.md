# Great Big Sea

Great Big Sea was my first favorite band. I had their album *Play!* on cassette, and it was essentially the soundtrack to my early childhood. To this day, it remains a deeply nostalgic, feel-good album for me, anchoring me back to a simpler time of discovery.
