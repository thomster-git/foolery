# Skrillex

Mixed into my punk rock and indie phases, I got heavily into dubstep, including Sonny Moore's early days as Skrillex and later Jack Ü. This music represents pure kinetic energy for me — it's the soundtrack to dancing for hours, moshing, and running on pure adrenaline and autistic energy.
