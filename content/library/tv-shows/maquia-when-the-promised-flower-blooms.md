# Maquia: When the Promised Flower Blooms

A 2018 Japanese animated fantasy film directed by Mari Okada. Maquia is a member of the Iorph, an immortal clan who weave a special cloth called hibiol — a tapestry of their memories and history. When her homeland is attacked, she flees and discovers an orphaned human baby named Erial, whom she raises as her own son.

The film is a profound meditation on what it means to love someone you will inevitably outlive. As a parent, it hits different — watching Maquia's bond with Erial grow through childhood, adolescence, and adulthood while she stays frozen in time is one of the most emotionally affecting things I've ever watched in animation. It earned every tear.
