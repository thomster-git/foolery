> **Taking charge of personal wellness, physiological systems, and medical self-advocacy.**

Managing physical and neurological health requires active self-advocacy, rejecting diagnostic overshadowing, and understanding how bodily systems interact. From severe obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP therapy to resolving chronic retinal complications and managing autistic burnout, health is foundational to long-term vitality.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Sleep Apnea & CPAP Therapy**: Treating structurally driven airway collapse with positive airway pressure to restore nocturnal oxygen saturation, eliminate daytime microsleeps, and protect cardiovascular and ocular health.
- **Medical Self-Advocacy**: Challenging weight stigma and clinical dismissal with objective physiological telemetry.
- **Neurological & Physical Recovery**: Building sustainable routines that respect sensory boundaries, energy reserves, and cognitive needs.
