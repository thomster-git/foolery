> **Taking charge of personal wellness, physiological systems, and medical self-advocacy.**

Managing physical health requires active self-advocacy, rejecting diagnostic overshadowing, and understanding how bodily systems interact. From severe obstructive sleep apnea and CPAP therapy to resolving chronic retinal complications, proactive care is foundational to long-term vitality.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Sleep Apnea & CPAP Therapy**: Treating structurally driven airway collapse with positive airway pressure to restore nocturnal oxygen saturation, eliminate daytime microsleeps, and protect cardiovascular and ocular health.
- **Medical Self-Advocacy**: Challenging weight stigma and clinical dismissal with objective physiological telemetry.
- **Systemic Recovery & Prevention**: Understanding how nocturnal oxygenation, blood pressure, and metabolic health connect across the body.
