# Health & Physical Well-being

> **Taking charge of personal wellness and medical advocacy.**

Managing personal physical and mental health requires active self-advocacy and understanding how bodily systems interact. From sleep apnea CPAP therapy to managing burnout, health is foundational to long-term vitality.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Sleep Apnea & CPAP Therapy**: Managing airway health with CPAP therapy to dramatically improve sleep quality and oxygen saturation.
- **Mental Health & Recovery**: Strategies for managing depression, burnout, and sensory overload.
- **Physical Habits**: Building sustainable physical routines that accommodate energy levels and cognitive needs.
