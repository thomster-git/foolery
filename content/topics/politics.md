# Politics & Nuance

I believe my political stance is mostly centrist.

It irritates me when things are seen through such a polarizing lens. For example, the abortion debate: everyone's background and upbringing will influence where they stand on the issue, and every opinion is valid.

I also believe we are at a point in society where we aren't allowed to question or study things anymore. For example, maybe there is more to transgender identity than what is shared publicly. I fear that if I was born in 2013 rather than 1993, I would be finding myself joining a trans community and "transitioning" to try and find a sense of belonging. I was just undiagnosed autistic+adhd, and would consider myself Autigendered.
