# Gender Studies & Societal Discourse

> **Reflecting on university gender studies, societal rhetoric, and lived experiences.**

Reflecting on taking Gender Studies in university, the course curriculum primarily focused on Women's Studies rather than a broad, multi-dimensional analysis of gender constructs across society.

## University Classroom Observations

One of the early class discussions centered on analyzing an engineering student event slogan (*"D-Day"*—an engineering tradition held on the first Friday of each semester). While most students understood the wordplay context, the lecture framework pushed a single rigid interpretation, demonstrating how academic discourse often enforces ideological consensus over open discussion.

## Modern Rhetoric & The Nuance of Pay Gaps

In modern political discourse, topics like the gender pay gap are often presented without essential statistical variables. When comparing raw averages without accounting for occupational risk, dangerous working conditions, overtime hours, and parental leave choices, complex economic discussions get reduced to simplistic slogans. 

As a neurodivergent and disabled individual who has navigated systemic hurdles firsthand, I value nuanced, data-driven dialogue over partisan rhetoric.
