> **Lessons learned the hard way on authority, accountability, and real-world collaboration.**

Formative moments shape how we evaluate systems, authority figures, and fairness. Rather than accepting institutional rules at face value, true growth comes from questioning arbitrary power structures, standing up for the people around us, and recognizing that real-world problem solving is built on collaboration rather than competition.
