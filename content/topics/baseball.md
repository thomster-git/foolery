# Baseball & Sports Analytics

> **The timeless strategy, statistics, and history of baseball.**

Baseball is a sport steeped in tradition, intricate strategy, and statistical depth. From tracking season box scores to collecting vintage rookie cards, baseball offers an endless domain for analytical curiosity.

## Exploration Areas

- **Sports Card Collecting**: Curating baseball cards with a focus on vintage rookies and rare inserts.
- **Statistical Analytics**: Following sabermetrics and player performance trends across MLB seasons.
- **Game Strategy**: Appreciation for pitching matchups, defensive shifts, and diamond tactics.
