I've known my way around a computer from a young age, but it wasn't until recently that I really did any actual IT work.

As a kid, I knew and would use 4chan, YouTube, MSN Messenger.

My typing was so slow when IMing people, I was bullied for it and my parents downloaded a typing trainer which I used and was super effective.

I was always interested in computer games. The consoles I grew up on were SNES, Game Boy Colour, N64, PS2, Xbox 360, PC Gaming.

I wasn't ever any good at SNES games, but I would love to watch my parents playing through the games.

I would also say I was pretty bad at games until I started playing online games with my friends in the Xbox 360 era. If I had to guess, it was my ADHD affecting my ability to understand mechanics or beat any games (especially efficiently).
