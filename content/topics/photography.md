# Photography & Visual Media

> **Freezing time through light and composition.**

Photography is the art of seeing—capturing landscapes, physical products, macro laser engravings, and life moments through deliberate composition and exposure control.

## Practice Areas

- **Macro & Product Photography**: Photographing custom 3D prints, laser-engraved slate coasters, and sports card conditions.
- **Landscape & Nature**: Documenting Newfoundland coastal landscapes, changing seasons, and outdoor environments.
- **Digital Image Processing**: Editing RAW files for natural color balance, contrast, and fine detail highlight.
