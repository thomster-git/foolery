# Video Editing & Content Creation

> **Crafting narrative and rhythm through visual cuts.**

Video editing combines storytelling, audio sync, color grading, and motion graphics to create compelling visual media. From editing early gameplay clips to producing polished video walkthroughs, video creation relies on pacing and flow.

## Key Techniques & Focus Areas

- **Nonlinear Editing Tools**: DaVinci Resolve and Adobe Premiere Pro for multi-track editing.
- **Audio Sync & Sound Design**: Matching vocal tracks with background ambiance and kinetic cuts.
- **Visual Storytelling**: Pacing edits to keep viewers engaged while conveying complex technical concepts cleanly.
