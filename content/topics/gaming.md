# Gaming & Interactive Culture

> **Interactive worlds, game mechanics, and hardware preservation.**

Gaming has been a lifelong companion, serving as a creative outlet, a social bridge, and a deep dive into interactive systems. From early 16-bit SNES titles to online Xbox 360 multiplayer and PlayStation 2 hardware modding, video games offer a unique lens into system design and human engagement.

## Exploration Areas

- **Retro Hardware Modding**: Preserving classic PS2, SNES, and N64 hardware through hard drive mods and homebrew software.
- **Gaming & Neurodiversity**: Examining how rapid feedback loops, immersive environments, and clear rule systems align with ADHD hyperfocus.
- **Classic Game Preservation**: Archiving physical discs and cartridges for future generations.
