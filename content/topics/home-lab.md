# Home Labs & Self-Hosting

> **Owning your infrastructure and data.**

Building a personal Home Lab provides an invaluable playground for mastering enterprise networking, virtualization, and self-hosted cloud services. Rather than relying entirely on third-party SaaS vendors, a home lab puts computing power back in your own hands.

## Key Architecture & Core Services

- **Proxmox VE Hypervisor**: Running KVM virtual machines and lightweight LXC containers for isolated service environments.
- **Network Isolation & Security**: Configuring VLANs, firewall rules, and VPN access for secure remote management.
- **Self-Hosted Productivity**: Running private media hubs, network storage, automated backups, and local development environments.
