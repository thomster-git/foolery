Nothing clarifies what actually matters in your life faster than sitting in a plastic chair under the low lights of a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), watching a pulse oximeter trace rise and fall on a screen while your phone buzzes in your pocket with a routine workplace question.

## Two Different Worlds on the Same Phone

Inside the NICU, every single number on the monitor matters, and the nurses and doctors move with quiet, practiced care. Then you glance down at an email thread from an office where people are treating an arbitrary spreadsheet deadline like a life-or-death crisis—or worse, treating your absence at a hospital like an scheduling inconvenience.

Experiencing that contrast firsthand permanently rewires your loyalty. I will still work my tail off to build reliable systems and pull a team out of a real infrastructure fire, but I will never again confuse a company's lack of planning with an emergency that belongs ahead of my family.
