Canada Fluorspar Inc. operated fluorspar ore extraction and processing facilities in St. Lawrence, Newfoundland. 

I started here doing QA/QC, moved to IT Coordination, and eventually left entirely disillusioned. 

### Why It's on the Shit List
- **The MSP Trap:** I inherited a completely undocumented server room and an infrastructure managed by a third-party MSP that was more interested in recurring billing than actually solving root causes.
- **Corporate Coldness:** Five days prior to my son being born—while we were dealing with the trauma of a NICU stay—the company suddenly entered receivership, showing zero regard for the people keeping the operation running.
- **KPI Manipulation:** Leadership frequently changed KPI reporting metrics specifically to avoid accountability, rather than addressing the actual operational bottlenecks.
- **Ransomware & Receivership:** Operating a remote mine site requires rock-solid systems thinking. Between a devastating Akira ransomware attack (that we had to clean up ourselves) and the ultimate financial collapse, CFI is the textbook example of what happens when you treat IT and workflow design as an afterthought.
