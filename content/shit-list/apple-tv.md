### Why It's on the Shit List
AppleTV+ is on the list for taking away my Friday Night Baseball. By buying up exclusive rights, they forced local broadcasts off the air, meaning you can't just throw on the Jays game on a Friday night without navigating an entirely different ecosystem and hitting an unnecessary paywall. It fragments the viewing experience and punishes the fans.
