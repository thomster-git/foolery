# Sports Cards & Baseball Memorabilia

Sports card collecting is currently on hold, mostly because of geography. Where I live in Newfoundland, there are no local card shows, few people who collect, and limited access to the in-person community that makes the hobby genuinely engaging.

For now I'm concentrating on **Blue Jays cards**, cool parallels, and cards I just genuinely like — collecting for personal enjoyment rather than investment or community validation. The hobby fell apart for me once before when the gatekeeping and clique energy of sports collecting culture felt exclusionary. This time I'm doing it on my own terms.

The best thing my dad did was get me started. That generosity matters more than any specific card.
