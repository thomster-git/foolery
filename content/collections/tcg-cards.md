# Yu-Gi-Oh! & Trading Card Games

I collected Yu-Gi-Oh! cards because I loved the strategic dueling mechanics. Pokémon cards were for everyone. Yu-Gi-Oh! felt like it rewarded thinking.

The biggest problem I had with playing was that I could tell when people were cheating — stacking the deck, manipulating the order of their draws — and I never knew how to navigate that. Some kids would tell you, with complete confidence, that stacking was actually how you were supposed to play. I knew they were lying. I didn't have the social tools to call it out effectively.

My favorite strategy before I stopped playing involved the **DNA card** (setting the type of every monster on the field to one type) combined with **Insect Repellent** (preventing insect-type monsters from attacking). I thought that was genuinely clever. I still do.

I also played exactly one Magic: The Gathering draft event. Won one game. Lost one game. My vision was so bad at the time that reading every card under bright fluorescent lights was genuinely unenjoyable — I was essentially playing half-blind, squinting at text I could only partially make out. Never went back.
