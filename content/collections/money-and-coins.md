# Canadian Coins & Numismatics

My father got me started in coin collecting — not through inheritance, but by sharing his own interest and introducing me to what makes coins worth noticing: mint marks, die varieties, composition changes, historical context.

The best piece I currently have is a **large bust Mountie quarter**. I'm still working through the collection systematically, learning what's there and what it means, and discovering new pieces along the way.

There's something meditative about numismatics — the careful examination, the reference books, the slow accumulation of knowledge. It rewards patience and attention to detail in a way that clicks naturally.
