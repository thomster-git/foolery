This project revolves around bringing my physical trading card collection into the digital realm. 

The collection has grown over the years, and it's time to properly sort, protect, and document it. By treating the collection as a digital archive, I can create a custom gallery experience on the site to showcase the best pieces.

### Project Goals
- **Physical Sorting**: Systematically sort the physical cards by set, rarity, and condition.
- **Photography & Scanning**: Take high-quality photos/scans of the most notable cards in the collection.
- **Digital Gallery**: Build a custom layout or gallery page on Project Atlas specifically designed to display the collection beautifully, complete with metadata for each card.

### Current Challenges & Starting Steps

The main hurdle in bringing this project to life is figuring out a reliable, repeatable workflow for capturing high-quality images of the cards. Trading cards—especially those with gloss or holofoil elements—are notoriously difficult to photograph consistently. 

Before building the digital gallery, the following starting steps need to be figured out:

1. **Lighting Setup:** Resolving how to capture foil/holographics without blowing out the image with glare. This will likely involve setting up a small, diffused lightbox or a specific flash-bounce technique.
2. **Standardization:** Establishing a fixed camera distance, angle, and a stark black or white backdrop so every card image looks perfectly uniform when displayed on the site.
3. **Data Entry Pipeline:** Creating a fast, frictionless way to log the card metadata (Set, Year, Rarity, Condition, etc.) alongside the image so that expanding the archive doesn't become a chore.
