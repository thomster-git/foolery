When the Ocarina of Time Remake officially drops, it is going to be a massive focus point. This project serves as the central hub for all the time and energy I plan to pour into it.

### Future Project Goals
- **Dedicated Playthroughs**: Streaming and recording the experience to capture the magic of returning to a reimagined Hyrule.
- **Tool Integration**: Expanding and utilizing the custom Ocarina Practice Tool built on the site.
- **Content Creation**: Deep dives, comparisons, and explorations of the new mechanics and world design compared to the N64 original.
