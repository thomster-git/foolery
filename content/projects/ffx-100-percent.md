This project is dedicated to achieving a true 100% completion run of Final Fantasy X. 

The goal isn't just to beat the game, but to conquer every single challenge it has to offer: maxing out the Sphere Grid, dodging 200 lightning bolts, completing the monster arena, obtaining every Celestial Weapon, and defeating Penance and the Dark Aeons.

### Project Goals
- **Full 100% Completion**: Every sidequest, every ultimate weapon, every super boss.
- **Documentation & Archiving**: The entire journey will be streamed/recorded and archived on YouTube.
- **Custom Tooling**: Leveraging the custom FFX Tracker tool built for Project Atlas to monitor progress in real-time.
