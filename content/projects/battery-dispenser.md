# Battery Dispenser & Workshop Storage

> **Gravity-fed, modular battery organization for home workshops.**

## Overview

Over the years, managing rechargeable AA, AAA, and 9V batteries across home lab devices, VR controllers, and workshop tools led to clutter. Instead of buying generic plastic bins, I designed a modular dispenser system manufactured using my **xTool S1 40W Diode Laser** and 3D printers.

## Design Goals & Mechanics

- **Gravity Loading & Dispensing**: Load fresh batteries from the top, draw oldest cells from the bottom tray.
- **Capacity**: Holds 50+ AA/AAA batteries in a compact footprint.
- **No Complex Hardware**: Snap-fit and interlocking finger-joint wood assembly requiring minimal glue.
- **Visual Appeal**: Laser-etched battery indicators and slate-accented base.

## Modular Variants

1. **Variant A (AAA Cells)**: Compact vertical gravity chute for AAA batteries.
2. **Variant B (AA Cells)**: Wide-channel chute designed for high-capacity AA packs.
3. **Variant C (Combo Modular)**: Dual-chamber dispenser with removable 3D-printed battery dividers.

## Commercial Plans

Once final tolerances are verified, vector SVG cutting templates and 3D STL files will be packaged for digital download on Etsy and maker platforms.
