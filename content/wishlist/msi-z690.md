**Must be purchased together with the [Intel Core i9-13900K](#13900k).**

When I was upgrading the server from 64GB to 128GB of RAM, the cooler springs jolted my old board as I removed it and bent several socket pins. This LGA 1700 DDR4 board replaces it directly while letting me keep my existing 128GB of DDR4-3200 MHz RAM—maximizing the build without migrating to DDR5—and gives me clean IOMMU groupings for GPU passthrough to a Windows 11 VM under Proxmox without hypervisor conflicts.
