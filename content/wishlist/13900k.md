**Must be purchased together with the [MSI MAG Z690 TOMAHAWK WIFI DDR4](#msi-z690).**

This upgrade started when I went to expand my home lab server from 64GB to 128GB of RAM. Pulling the cooler off, the mounting springs jolted the old motherboard and bent socket pins. Rather than throwing good money after a dead board, replacing the motherboard and CPU together with an i9-13900K (24 cores: 8P + 16E, LGA 1700, Intel UHD Graphics 770) lets me carry over all 128GB of DDR4-3200 MHz RAM and max out the platform without buying a whole new DDR5 kit.
