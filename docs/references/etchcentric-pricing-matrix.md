# EtchCentric Creations — Coaster Pricing & Production Economics

This document details the pricing strategy, machine settings, production economics, and Canadian competitive market analysis for EtchCentric Creations' slate and acacia + marble coaster product lines.

---

## 1. Pricing Structure & Tiers

### Tier 1: Zero-Prep Stock Vectors & Craft Fair Clearance Slate
* **Applicable Products:** Slate Coasters — Square & Circle
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Coaster:** $12.00 CAD
  * **Set of 4:** $35.00 CAD ($8.75/coaster — Save $13.00 CAD vs 4 singles)
* **Scope:** Pre-made vector files (sports teams, holiday icons, geometric art, classic monograms) or pre-engraved craft fair inventory.
* **Production & Economics:**
  * **Prep Time:** ~2 minutes to position vector in xTool Creative Space / Studio.
  * **Machine Time (xTool S1 40W):** ~5 minutes per coaster vector-fill (~20 minutes for a 4-pack).
  * **Labor Return:**
    * Singles: **$360.00 CAD/active prep hour** ($102.86 CAD/total cycle hour).
    * Sets of 4: **$700.00 CAD/active prep hour** ($91.30 CAD/total cycle hour).
* **Market Benchmarks:**
  * Memories Made CA: $10.00 base + $5.00 graphic = $15.00 CAD single / $37.00 CAD 4-pack.
  * 911 Duty Gear Canada: $31.99 CAD sale ($39.99 CAD reg).
  * US Imported Sports Coasters: $72.00 CAD + $42.95 shipping ($114.95 CAD delivered).

### Tier 2: Bespoke 1-of-1 Customer Photo Slate
* **Applicable Products:** Slate Coasters — Square & Circle
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Coaster:** $15.00 CAD
  * **Set of 4 (1 Shared Photo):** $45.00 CAD ($11.25/coaster — Save $15.00 CAD vs 4 singles)
  * **Set of 4 (4 Unique Photos):** $55.00 CAD ($13.75/coaster)
* **Scope:** Customer-submitted photographs (pets, family portraits, custom photography).
* **Production & Economics:**
  * **Prep Time:** ~20 minutes active editing per photo (background removal, cropping, gamma/contrast curve tuning, error-diffusion dithering for slate).
  * **Machine Time (xTool S1 40W):** ~20 minutes high-DPI raster pass per 4-inch slate coaster.
  * **Labor Return:**
    * 4 Unique Photos ($55 CAD for 80m prep + 80m machine time): **$41.25 CAD/active prep hour** ($20.62 CAD/total cycle hour).
  * **Strategic Gap:** A strict **$10 to $20 CAD premium** above Tier 1 protects evening hours from low-margin photo preparation and fairly compensates high-DPI laser passes.

---

### Tier 3: Custom Acacia + Marble Coasters
* **Applicable Products:** Marble & Acacia Coasters — Round & Square
* **Pricing:**
  * **Single Coaster:** $16.00 CAD
  * **Set of 4 (Shared Design / Monogram):** $48.00 CAD ($12.00/coaster — Save $16.00 CAD vs 4 singles)
  * **Set of 4 (4 Unique Names / Styles):** $54.00 CAD ($13.50/coaster)
* **Scope:** Premium dual-material coasters pairing cool white natural marble with warm acacia wood grain.
* **Production & Economics:**
  * **Machine Settings (xTool S1 40W):** Acacia wood section engraves at **200–230 mm/s** at **20%–25% power** in 1 pass (~4 minutes per coaster / 16 minutes for set of 4).
  * **Prep Time:** ~10 minutes layout for shared family monogram; ~15 minutes for 4 unique names/coordinates.
  * **Labor Return:**
    * Shared Monogram ($48 CAD): **$288.00 CAD/active prep hour** ($110.77 CAD/total cycle hour).
    * Highest machine-minute profit margin in the workshop.
* **Market Benchmarks:**
  * Blank engravables: $14.25 CAD + $14.90 shipping ($29.15 CAD delivered).
  * Personalized 4-pack: $49.99 CAD retail.
  * Gift bundles (cutting board + coaster sets): $53.00 CAD + $10.00 shipping ($63.00 CAD delivered).
