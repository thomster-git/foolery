# Article Page Specification

> **Detailed view for individual long-form articles, essays, and stories.**

---

## Overview

The Article Page presents deep-dive content. Rather than an isolated blog post, an article in Atlas is an interconnected node linking back to primary topics, themes, related projects, and adjacent stories.

---

## Page Architecture

1. **Header & Breadcrumbs**: Displays context hierarchy (`Home / Articles / [Article Title]`).
2. **Metadata Bar**: Shows creation/update dates, reading time, author, and status badge.
3. **Article Body**: Formatted markdown content with styled typography, callouts, and inline cross-links.
4. **Connections Section**:
   - **Topics**: Badges linking to parent topic pages.
   - **Themes**: Badges highlighting abstract themes explored in the article.
   - **Related Content**: Card recommendations for further exploration.

---

## Design Goals

- **Distraction-Free Reading**: Clear line height, high-contrast typography, readable max-width.
- **Serendipitous Exploration**: Related topic and theme links encourage the reader to continue exploring after finishing the article.
