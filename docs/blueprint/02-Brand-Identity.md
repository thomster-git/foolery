# Brand Overview

Thoms Foolery is a digital home where technology, creativity, nostalgia, and personal growth intersect through connected stories, projects, and ideas.

It is designed to encourage exploration rather than consumption.

Visitors are invited to follow their curiosity, discover unexpected connections, and experience a personal website that feels less like a collection of pages and more like a place worth exploring.

---

# Brand Personality

If Thoms Foolery were a person, it would be...

- Curious
- Thoughtful
- Authentic
- Playful
- Analytical
- Creative
- Humble
- Occasionally sarcastic
- Optimistic
- Calm

The brand should never feel corporate, performative, or driven by trends.

Instead, it should feel genuine, approachable, and built by someone who enjoys learning just as much as teaching.

---

# Brand Values

Thoms Foolery values...

- Curiosity
- Learning
- Craftsmanship
- Authenticity
- Creativity
- Exploration
- Connection

These values should be reflected throughout the website, regardless of topic or medium.

---

# Voice & Tone

Thoms Foolery speaks like a knowledgeable friend.

It explains ideas with enthusiasm while remaining approachable, honest, and respectful.

The tone should encourage curiosity rather than claim authority.

Humour is welcome.

Arrogance is not.

Complex ideas should be explained clearly without talking down to the audience.

Whenever possible, the brand should invite exploration instead of simply providing answers.

---

# Writing Style

Writing should be conversational, thoughtful, and intentional.

Stories should teach ideas.

Technical content should remain accessible.

Creative content should remain authentic.

Articles should encourage readers to think, reflect, and continue exploring beyond the page.

Whenever possible, writing should connect ideas together rather than treating them as isolated topics.

---

# Visual Identity

The visual identity of Thoms Foolery should reinforce its philosophy.

Layouts should encourage exploration.

Whitespace should improve readability.

Animations should communicate relationships rather than exist purely for decoration.

Visual hierarchy should guide curiosity rather than demand attention.

The interface should feel modern without becoming trendy, expressive without becoming distracting, and playful without sacrificing clarity.

---

# Left • Middle • Right Lenses

The Left, Middle, and Right sections are not separate websites.

They are different lenses through which visitors can experience the same ideas.

## Left Lens

Prioritizes objective thinking.

Characteristics include:

- Technical depth
- Engineering
- Data
- Analysis
- Logic
- Systems
- Documentation
- Timelines

The visual style should feel structured, minimal, and precise.

---

## Right Lens

Prioritizes subjective thinking.

Characteristics include:

- Stories
- Creativity
- Emotion
- Reflection
- Memories
- Photography
- Music
- Art

The visual style should feel expressive, colourful, and organic.

---

## Middle Lens

Represents the balance between both perspectives.

Facts supported by stories.

Stories supported by facts.

This should become the default experience throughout most of Thoms Foolery.

Rather than choosing between logic and creativity, the Middle Lens demonstrates how both strengthen one another.

---

# Colour Philosophy

Colours should communicate meaning before aesthetics.

The Left Lens should favour cooler, restrained colours that reinforce precision and structure.

The Right Lens should favour warmer, more expressive colours that reinforce creativity and emotion.

The Middle Lens should blend both approaches into a balanced, welcoming experience.

Specific colour palettes are defined within the Design System.

---

# Typography

Typography should prioritize readability above all else.

Different typefaces may be used to reinforce the personalities of the Left and Right lenses, but readability and accessibility should never be compromised.

Typography should communicate confidence without feeling rigid and personality without becoming distracting.

Detailed font selections are defined within the Design System.

---

# Iconography

Icons should be simple, recognizable, and purposeful.

They should communicate ideas quickly while remaining visually consistent throughout the website.

Decorative icons should support storytelling rather than replace it.

---

# Photography & Media

Photography should feel personal rather than commercial.

Images should communicate authenticity, craftsmanship, and genuine experiences.

Where possible, original photography should be prioritized over stock imagery.

Media should support stories rather than simply decorate them.

---

# Motion & Animation

Motion should reinforce understanding.

Animations should help visitors understand relationships, transitions, and hierarchy.

Movement should feel smooth, intentional, and restrained.

Animation exists to improve the experience, never to distract from it.

---

# Accessibility

Accessibility is a core part of the Thoms Foolery identity.

Every visitor should be able to comfortably explore the website regardless of device, input method, or ability.

Accessibility should be considered during every stage of design rather than added afterward.

---

# Relationship to Atlas

The Brand Identity transforms the philosophy established in the Project Vision into a recognizable personality and visual language.

Every design decision should reinforce the principles defined in the Project Vision while remaining consistent with the identity described here.

Brand identity is not limited to colours or logos.

It is the complete expression of how Thoms Foolery communicates with its audience.
